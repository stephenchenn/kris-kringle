A few things to keep the magic alive +

Keep your invite link to yourself. It gives access to your wishlist and recipients.

Reserve thoughtfully. Only reserve gifts you intend to buy. The recipient can’t see reservations; their gift givers can, so there are no accidental doubles.

Give them options. Add a few ideas at different prices. You can add more items than the number of gifts you’re receiving.
